# OpenAI result-family source review · 2026-10-13

[Problem index](README.md)

This source review maps 23 selected result families to catalog statements. It is a catalog maintenance record, not an independent Lean verification or an award decision. All proof references use OpenAI Math snapshot [`1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a`](https://github.com/openai/math/commit/1581d4cbdd69a0faf34b2089614fe2a814316a6a). The snapshot is the source repository tip captured for this review; it is not asserted to be the original release commit.

The update adds 22 records, JSP-001051–JSP-001072, and refreshes the existing Crouzeix record JSP-000042. All 22 additions record qualified published full-solution manuscript claims as Solved, with independent mathematical review pending. Nineteen also record claimed complete formalizations as Yes, with eligibility Pending verification. Three have Lean proof No because the selected targets cover only narrower results; their mathematical manuscript claims and formalization coverage are assessed separately. The pre-existing unit-distance solution and its contributors are preserved. Original statements of related broader problems are unchanged.

## Evidence and limits

Reviewed: pinned manuscript descriptions and authorship records, formalization scope pages, Comparator configurations and challenge statements, selected solution modules, and the Vlasov–Maxwell admissible-data definition. The project declares Lean `leanprover/lean4:v4.34.1` and pinned Lake dependencies. Its metadata labels project scope as partial progress and review status as unchecked; family-level scope must therefore be assessed separately.

Not completed: independent mathematical review, a clean build of the selected targets with that exact toolchain and dependency set, transitive `#print axioms` checks, kernel replay, or challenge/Comparator execution. Configuration files listing permitted axioms do not demonstrate the actual axiom dependencies. Placeholder proofs in challenge statements define intended targets; they are not the submitted solution and do not alone establish a proof gap. This review cannot certify any of the 23 submissions as independently verified complete Lean proofs.

Solved/Yes below follow the existing catalog convention for explicitly qualified published complete-result claims. They do not start public review, register a candidate, confer priority, establish a recipient or authorize a reward. The new claimed-complete records and the refreshed Crouzeix record have eligibility Pending verification and both claim roles Unavailable. The three uncovered broader records have eligibility No. MathDB cohort membership and category registration are unchanged.
